Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ last_updated }}

This Privacy Policy explains how Hairship (“Hairship”, “we”, “us”) handles information in Hairship Operations, the internal salon operations app available at operation.hairship.in (the “App”). The App is used only by Hairship employees.

1. Information we collect

Staff account information

For each employee we store the name, username, role, branch, a securely hashed password, and records of the work done in the App, such as service steps started and completed, timings, and review follow-up notes.

Salon operations information

Service orders and visit records created by branch managers, including invoice numbers, the services provided, the staff assigned, and daily sales reports uploaded by the branch.

Information from Google Business Profile

When the Hairship account that owns our Google Business Profile listings authorises the App, our review service uses the Google Business Profile API to access, for Hairship’s own locations only:

  • the account and location identifiers of each Hairship branch listing;
  • reviews posted on those locations: star rating, review text, reviewer display name (or “Anonymous”), review creation and update times, and the review identifier;
  • any existing owner reply to a review.

We do not access reviewers’ email addresses, contact details or Google accounts, and we do not access any listing that Hairship does not own or manage.

2. How we use information

  • To let staff run salon services to a standard procedure and to produce branch reports.
  • To show each Google review to the Hairship branch it belongs to, identify the issues or praise it mentions, and let the branch manager record follow-up with the customer and staff.
  • To prepare and publish Hairship’s reply to a review on Google, as the owner’s response for that branch.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Hairship Operations’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the review features described above, which are visible and useful to Hairship staff.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of reviewers.
  • Google user data is not transferred to others except to the service providers that host and process data for the App under confidentiality obligations, when required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice.
  • Google user data is not used to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Automated analysis is used only to classify and reply to Hairship’s own reviews.
  • No person reads Google user data except Hairship staff handling review follow-up, or where needed for security, to comply with law, or with the account owner’s consent.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Hairship staff who need it for their role, with our hosting and cloud providers who process it on our behalf, and with authorities where the law requires it. Replies we publish to Google reviews are public on Google, as all owner responses are.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers and accessed over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access inside the App is limited by role and branch. Google OAuth tokens are held only by our review service in a secret store, are never shown in the App, and are used only for the purposes above.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep review and operations records for as long as they are needed for branch follow-up, reporting and our legal obligations. When a review is removed from Google, or when the Google account owner revokes access, we stop retrieving new data. The account owner can revoke the App’s access at any time from Google Account permissions. To ask us to delete stored data, email {{ contact_email }} and we will delete it within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Your choices

Employees can ask to see or correct their account information through their branch manager or by email. If you posted a review on a Hairship listing, you can edit or delete it on Google at any time; you can also email us to ask about information we hold about your review.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be communicated to staff in the App.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: {{ contact_email }}.
