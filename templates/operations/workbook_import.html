This updates global services, sub-services, tasks, inventory, equipment and their mappings. It does not create staff, branches, customers or visits. Existing visit task snapshots are preserved. Rows absent from the workbook are not deleted.

1. Download the current SOP

Get every service, step, task, inventory and equipment row as it is right now, including changes made in the service catalogue. Use it for bulk changes, such as adding a step to every facial or adding many services at once.

The Read me sheet explains how to add, move and switch off rows. Grey columns are only for reading and filtering.

Download SOP workbook

2. Upload and validate

Choose your edited .xlsx workbook (maximum 10 MB and 10,000 rows). Validation shows what will change and saves nothing yet.

If the catalogue changed after you downloaded the workbook, the upload stops so it cannot undo someone else's change. Download a fresh copy and redo your change.
